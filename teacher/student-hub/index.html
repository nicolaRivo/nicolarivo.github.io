Student Hub

A practice hub for every student.

Every student gets a private online hub: their songs with separate instrument tracks to play along with, weekly practice notes, and a growing library of licks, voicings and patterns. It works on phone, tablet and computer.

Try the demo Book a trial lesson

What’s inside

Everything for the week, in one place.

  1. 01

    Songs, track by track

    The songs we’re working on, split into separate tracks: vocals, guitar, piano, bass, drums. Mute your instrument and play with the band, solo a part to hear it clearly, slow it down without changing the pitch and loop the tricky bar. Chords, lyrics and notes from the lesson sit alongside.

    • Mute & solo
    • Slow down
    • Loop
    • Chords & lyrics
  2. 02

    This week

    After each lesson the hub shows what to practise: a longer piece of work on a song, and a short daily routine we call “brushing your teeth”. Each task links straight to the song or library entry it’s about.

    • Weekly notes
    • Daily routine
    • Linked to songs
  3. 03

    Your library

    Licks, voicings and patterns, written out and playable: notation in any of the 12 keys, a keyboard or fretboard that lights up as it plays, scale degrees and Roman numerals to show why it works, and real piano and guitar sounds. It grows as you learn.

    • 12 keys
    • Keyboard & fretboard
    • Chord shapes
    • Theory made visible

Private by design

Only for the student.

A real student’s hub opens only with their own password, and the songs and notes inside are encrypted: the link on its own doesn’t open anything. Students can stay signed in on their own phone or computer.
